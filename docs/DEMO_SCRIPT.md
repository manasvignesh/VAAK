# VAAK — 3-Minute Fourth-Wall-Breaking Demo Script

**Target Duration:** `2:48` (Safe inside `2:45 – 2:55` for the 3-minute limit)  
**Tone:** Direct, human, conversational — speak **to the evaluator**, not *at* a slide deck.  
**Pair With:** `VAAK_Demo_Master_2m50s.mp4` (record your own voice over the visual master in 1 take — real human emotion beats any synthetic voice).

---

## 0:00 – 0:22 | Hook: Break the Fourth Wall (Home Screen)
- **On Screen:** Opening title card → Home screen + 3D Voice Sculpture reacting.
- **Say to the Evaluator:**
  > "Before I show you a single feature, try this for two seconds: imagine you're sitting in a crowded Indian lecture hall, and you can't hear the professor—or you can't speak up to ask a doubt—or you can't see the board at all.  
  > Most accessibility tools treat those as three completely separate apps. So students end up juggling broken workarounds while the class moves on without them.  
  > We built **VAAK** so that never happens again."

---

## 0:22 – 1:15 | Live Classroom: Real Mic → Captions, Voice Marks & Native Translation
- **On Screen:** `Start Live Class` → Consent → `Connected to Deepgram Nova-3` → live microphone streaming teacher speech → `"VAAK. Mark that as important."` → Telugu translation → Focus Mode.
- **Say to the Evaluator:**
  > "Watch what happens the moment class starts. This isn't pre-recorded text—this is live microphone audio streaming straight into VAAK.  
  > If you're deaf or hard-of-hearing, you don't just get captions—you get **confidence indicators** that highlight when a word might be unclear, so you're never misled by a bad transcription.  
  > When the teacher says something exam-critical, a simple **'VAAK, mark that as important'** flags it automatically.  
  > And if English isn't your comfort language? One tap gives you Telugu or Hindi—right next to the original English, so you never lose the technical terms."

---

## 1:15 – 1:35 | Student Participation: Raising Your Hand Without a Voice
- **On Screen:** `Participate` screen → typing *"Could you explain how chlorophyll absorbs light?"* → `Speak Aloud` + Quick Classroom Phrases.
- **Say to the Evaluator:**
  > "Now switch seats. Suppose you're a non-speaking student, and you have a doubt right now—before the teacher erases the board.  
  > You tap one quick phrase, or type your question, hit **Speak Aloud**, and VAAK speaks for you clearly in the classroom—while automatically pausing the class mic so it never triggers itself."

---

## 1:35 – 2:22 | Voice Companion: Eyes-Free Navigation & Grounded Q&A
- **On Screen:** `Activate Voice Mode` → `STATE: LISTENING` → spoken `"Open the latest class."` → `"What did the teacher explain about photosynthesis?"` → `"Explain that in Telugu."`
- **Say to the Evaluator:**
  > "Now close your eyes for a moment—because this is built for a blind or low-vision student.  
  > With Voice Mode on, you control the entire app hands-free. You open your latest class by voice, ask **'What did the teacher explain about photosynthesis?'**, and notice something crucial: VAAK refuses to hallucinate. Every answer is grounded strictly in your teacher's actual transcript, with the exact timestamp—and if you say **'Explain that in Telugu'**, it answers in Telugu right away."

---

## 2:22 – 2:50 | Evidence-Linked Study Notes & Closing
- **On Screen:** `My Classes` → `Smart Notes` → Core Takeaway, Key Concepts, Important Definitions with clickable `#seg-001` timestamps → Home closing shot.
- **Say to the Evaluator:**
  > "And when the bell rings, you don't leave empty-handed. VAAK turns the lecture into structured, evidence-linked study notes where every takeaway links back to the exact second your teacher said it.  
  > Speak. Understand. Participate. That's **VAAK**—communication without barriers. Thank you."
